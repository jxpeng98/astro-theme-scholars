## Course focus

This fictional course introduces research methods through invented civic settings, organizations, and public-interest scenarios.

## Methods

Simulated student teams practice interview design, observation, survey planning, trace analysis, and collaborative synthesis using generated materials.

## Final work

Fictional teams produce a scoped research plan with a synthetic evidence strategy and communication plan for an imaginary partner.
