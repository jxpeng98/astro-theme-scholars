## Course focus

The course introduced research methods for civic technology settings where public value, partner capacity, and research rigor must be considered together.

## Methods

Students practiced interview design, field observation, survey planning, trace analysis, and collaborative synthesis. Exercises focused on choosing methods that matched both the question and the realities of a partner organization.

## Final work

Teams produced a scoped research plan with an evidence strategy, ethical considerations, and a communication plan for partner review.
