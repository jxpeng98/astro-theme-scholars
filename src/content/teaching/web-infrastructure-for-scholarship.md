## Course premise

Researchers increasingly publish software, data, and explanatory interfaces on the web. This course introduces the infrastructure decisions that make those outputs durable and understandable.

## Topics

The course covers semantic HTML, content modeling, static generation, serverless functions, accessibility, deployment, and documentation. Technical choices are evaluated against the needs of scholarly maintenance and reuse.

## Assessment

Students incrementally build and document a small scholarly web tool. The final submission includes the deployed interface, source code, content workflow, and a short maintenance plan.
