## Course premise

This fictional studio uses invented systems, institutions, and stakeholder scenarios to demonstrate a course-detail layout.

## Learning goals

The fictional learning goals cover interpretability comparisons, participatory evaluation plans, imaginary policy constraints, and clear communication of system tradeoffs.

## Learning format

Weekly demo meetings combine close reading, critique, and short design exercises. The final fictional project evaluates a simulated system using generated evidence and invented stakeholder perspectives.
