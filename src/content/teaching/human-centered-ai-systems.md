## Course premise

AI systems are shaped by technical choices, institutional conditions, and the people affected by their deployment. This seminar gives students a structured way to study those relationships together.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, students can compare interpretability methods, plan participatory evaluation, identify policy constraints, and communicate system tradeoffs to technical and non-technical audiences.

## Learning format

Weekly meetings combine close reading, deployment critique, and short design exercises. The final project asks teams to evaluate a real or proposed AI system with both technical evidence and stakeholder perspectives.
