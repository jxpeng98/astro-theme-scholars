## Context

The lab needed to inspect social learning signals while also understanding where each observation came from. Existing dashboards separated visualization from collection history, which made anomalies difficult to investigate.

## Design response

I organized the interface around a traceable path from overview to community, time period, and source record. Data quality states were presented alongside the analytical views instead of being hidden in a separate administration tool.

## Reflection

The project reinforced a useful principle for research software: provenance is part of the interaction design. A polished chart is only trustworthy when a researcher can inspect the conditions that produced it.
