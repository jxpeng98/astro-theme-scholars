## Context

This fictional lab scenario needed to inspect generated learning signals while also showing where each synthetic observation came from.

## Design response

The demo interface follows a traceable path from overview to imaginary community, simulated time period, and generated source record. Data quality states appear alongside the analytical views.

## Reflection

The fictional case study illustrates one principle: provenance is part of interaction design, even when every record is synthetic.
